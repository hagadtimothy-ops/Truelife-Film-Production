GET TO KNOW OUR COMPANY

About
True Life.

True Life Film Production is a photography and videography company based in Sorsogon City, dedicated to capturing meaningful moments through creative visuals and storytelling.

We specialize in preserving life's important occasions through professional photography and cinematic video production. From portraits and birthdays to weddings, events, and documentaries, we aim to transform every moment into lasting memories.

With creativity, passion, and attention to detail, we strive to deliver visual experiences that tell stories worth remembering.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission.

To capture life's most meaningful moments through creative photography and cinematic storytelling. We are committed to preserving genuine emotions, meaningful connections, and unforgettable experiences through visuals crafted with passion, creativity, and attention to detail.

WHAT WE ASPIRE TO BE

Our Vision.

To become a trusted and recognized photography and videography team known for creating timeless visual stories. We aspire to inspire people through our work, build meaningful connections with every client, and preserve memories that can be treasured for generations.
